## 2. Providers you connect yourself

Some providers are not our sub-processors. If you connect a provider with your own account, it processes data under your own agreement with it. This includes your own AI, speech-to-text, text-to-speech or observability providers, such as OpenAI, Deepgram, ElevenLabs or Langfuse, and telephony accounts you bring, such as Twilio, Telnyx, Vonage or Plivo. You choose those providers and are responsible for your agreements with them.

## 3. How we notify changes

We give customers at least 30 days' notice by email before we add or replace a sub-processor. You may object to the change on reasonable grounds within that notice period, as set out in our [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa). This page always shows the current list and the date it was last updated.
