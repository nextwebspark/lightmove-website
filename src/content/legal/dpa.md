This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") sets out how Lightmove processes personal data on behalf of its customers, as Article 28 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) requires.

## 1. Parties and incorporation

This DPA is between you, the customer, as the controller, and Nextwebspark Limited, trading as Lightmove, a company registered in Ireland (company number 779540) with its registered office at 48 The Woods, Ros Mór, Crossagalla, Limerick, V94 8D51, Ireland ("Lightmove", "we", "us"), as the processor.

This DPA forms part of our [Terms of Service](/terms) and applies automatically when you use Lightmove to process personal data. If this DPA and the Terms conflict on data protection, this DPA wins. Words such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor", "data subject" and "personal data breach" have the meaning the GDPR gives them.

## 2. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose

- **Subject matter:** providing the Lightmove service, which lets you build, test and run AI voice agents that answer and make phone and web calls.
- **Duration:** for as long as you use the service, and until we have deleted or returned your personal data under section 4.10.
- **Nature:** hosting, storing, recording, transcribing, analysing with AI models, transmitting and deleting personal data.
- **Purpose:** to provide, secure and support the service for you, following your instructions.

Annex I gives the details of processing.

## 3. Personal data and data subjects

- **Types of personal data:** call audio, call transcripts, caller phone numbers, contact lists you upload for call campaigns, knowledge-base content you upload, and agent configuration such as prompts and workflows, to the extent these contain personal data.
- **Data subjects:** people who call your agents or are called by them, the contacts in your campaign lists, and your users of the Lightmove app.
- **Special category data:** you should not ask callers for special category data unless you have a lawful basis for it and have told us. [CONFIRM POSITION ON SPECIAL CATEGORY DATA]

## 4. Our obligations as processor

### 4.1 Instructions

We process personal data only on your documented instructions, including on transfers outside the EEA, unless EU or Irish law requires otherwise. If so, we tell you before processing unless that law forbids it. Your instructions are these Terms and this DPA, and how you configure and use the service. We tell you if we think an instruction breaks data protection law.

### 4.2 Confidentiality

Everyone we authorise to process personal data is bound by confidentiality, by contract or by law.

### 4.3 Security

We take the technical and organisational measures Article 32 GDPR requires, set out in Annex II. We may update them as long as the overall level of protection does not fall.

### 4.4 Sub-processors

You give us general authorisation to use sub-processors. Our current sub-processors are listed on our [Sub-processors](/subprocessors) page (Annex III). We give you at least 30 days' notice by email before we add or replace a sub-processor. You may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If we cannot address your objection, you may end the affected service before the change takes effect. We put data protection terms in place with each sub-processor that protect personal data at least as well as this DPA, and we remain responsible to you for their work.

Providers you connect with your own account, such as your own AI, speech or telephony providers, are not our sub-processors. They process data under your agreement with them.

### 4.5 Data subject requests

Taking into account the nature of the processing, we help you, by appropriate technical and organisational measures, to answer requests from data subjects exercising their GDPR rights. If a data subject contacts us directly about data we process for you, we pass the request to you and do not answer it ourselves unless you ask us to.

### 4.6 Assistance with your compliance

We help you meet your obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR (security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation), taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to us.

### 4.7 Personal data breaches

We notify you without undue delay, and in any case within [48] hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your personal data. We give you the information you reasonably need to meet your own notification duties, as it becomes available, and take reasonable steps to contain the breach.

### 4.8 Records

We keep the records of processing Article 30(2) GDPR requires.

### 4.9 Audits and information

We make available the information needed to show compliance with Article 28 GDPR, and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, by you or an auditor you appoint. Audits need reasonable notice, take place during business hours, must not disrupt the service or breach other customers' confidentiality, and are at your cost. ### 4.10 Deletion or return

- **Call content:** recordings, transcripts and other call content are deleted automatically after the retention period you set, from 1 to 365 days. The default is 90 days.
- **Account data:** when your account is closed, we delete your account data within 30 days, unless law requires us to keep it.
- **Backups:** our database backups are deleted after 30 days, so deleted data leaves them within 30 days.

At your request before closure, we return your personal data to you. [CONFIRM HOW DATA IS RETURNED OR EXPORTED]

## 5. International transfers

Your personal data is hosted in the EU, on Google Cloud in Belgium (europe-west1). We transfer personal data outside the EEA only where the transfer is protected by an adequacy decision, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients, or by Standard Contractual Clauses. Where a transfer from you to us, or from us to a sub-processor, needs them, the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission in Decision 2021/914 are incorporated into this DPA by reference: Module 2 (controller to processor) and Module 3 (processor to processor). Annexes I and II of this DPA complete their annexes.

## 6. Your obligations

You are responsible for having a lawful basis for the processing you instruct, for telling callers about recording and AI agents where the law requires, and for the accuracy of the personal data you give us.

## Annex II: Technical and organisational measures

- **EU hosting:** the app and its data are hosted on Google Cloud in Belgium (europe-west1).
- **Encryption in transit:** traffic between browsers or apps and our servers is encrypted with TLS.
- **Encryption at rest:** data is encrypted at rest on Google Cloud.
- **Secrets:** carrier credentials, API keys and integration secrets are additionally encrypted at rest in our database.
- **Network:** the database sits on a private network.
- **Storage:** our storage bucket is private, with public access prevention turned on. Files such as recordings are shared only through short-lived signed links that expire after at most one hour.
- **Tenant isolation:** each customer's data is separated by organisation.
- **Access control in the app:** role-based access controls what each user in an organisation can do.
- **Retention:** call content is deleted automatically when the customer's retention period ends.
- **Logging:** our logs mask phone numbers and email addresses.
- **Staff access:** access to personal data is limited to staff who need it.
