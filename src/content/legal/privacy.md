This policy explains what personal data Lightmove collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers our website, the Lightmove app at app.lightmove.ai, and the voice agents our customers run on it.

## 1. Who we are

Lightmove is the trading name of Nextwebspark Limited, a company registered in Ireland (company number 779540) with its registered office at 48 The Woods, Ros Mór, Crossagalla, Limerick, V94 8D51, Ireland. For account and website data we are the data controller. For call data that our customers' agents handle, we act as a data processor on our customer's behalf.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, work email, company and login details.
- **Call data:** audio recordings, transcripts, caller phone numbers, call summaries and anything callers say to an agent.
- **Agent configuration:** the prompts, workflows and settings you create.
- **Billing details:** handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.
- **Usage data:** basic logs about how the website and app are used. Our website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser, only if you pick one. Our hosting provider keeps server logs, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. ## 3. How we use it

- To run the service: answer calls, run your workflows and show you call logs.
- To support you, bill you and keep the service secure.
- To improve the product, using aggregated or de-identified data where we can.

We do not sell personal data. We never use customer data, including call recordings and transcripts, to train AI models.

## 4. Google user data

You can connect a Google account so your agents can work with your calendar, spreadsheets and email. We only request access when you connect an integration, and only use it for the features you set up.

| Google service           | What Lightmove does with it                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google Calendar          | Check availability and create, move or cancel events when a caller books.                     |
| Google Sheets            | Read and write rows in sheets you choose, for example to log calls or look up prices.         |
| Gmail (send only)        | Send emails you have set up in a workflow, such as call summaries. We cannot read your inbox. |
| Google Drive (app files) | Access only files Lightmove creates or you open with it.                                      |
| Email address            | Identify which Google account is connected.                                                   |

Lightmove's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not let people read it unless you ask us to, it is needed for security, or the law requires it. You can disconnect at any time in the app or at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## 5. Demo calls on our website

If you press **Talk to Aoife** on our home page, you talk to an AI voice agent and we are the controller of that call. We record your voice and make a transcript to run the demo and improve it, and we log technical data such as your IP address and browser. The legal basis is the consent you give by starting the call. You can end the call at any time, and you can ask us to delete the recording by emailing [privacy@lightmove.ai](mailto:privacy@lightmove.ai). The audio is processed on Google Cloud in the EU. ## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your personal data, and object to how we use it. Email us to make a request. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the [Data Protection Commission](https://www.dataprotection.ie) in Ireland.

If you called a business that uses Lightmove, that business controls your call data. Contact them first. We will help them answer your request.

## 9. Security and transfers

Data is encrypted in transit and access is limited to staff who need it. The app and its data are hosted on Google Cloud in the EU (Belgium, europe-west1). Where data leaves the EEA, we use Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful safeguard.
