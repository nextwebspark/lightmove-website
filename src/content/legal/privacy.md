This policy explains what personal data Lightmove collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers our website, the Lightmove app at app.lightmove.ai, and the voice agents our customers run on it.

## 1. Who we are

Lightmove is the trading name of Nextwebspark Limited, a company registered in Ireland (company number 779540) with its registered office at 48 The Woods, Ros Mór, Crossagalla, Limerick, V94 8D51, Ireland. For account and website data we are the data controller. For call data that our customers' agents handle, we act as a data processor on our customer's behalf, under our [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa).

## 2. What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, work email, company and login details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
- **Call data:** audio recordings, transcripts, caller phone numbers, call summaries and anything callers say to an agent. Our customers also upload contact lists for outbound call campaigns.
- **Agent configuration:** the prompts, workflows and settings you create.
- **Billing details:** payments are handled by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.
- **Usage data:** basic logs about how the website and app are used. Our website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser, only if you pick one. Our hosting provider keeps server logs, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. The app uses only the cookies and browser storage needed to sign you in and remember your display preferences, such as theme and sidebar. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

## 3. How we use it, and our legal basis

- **To provide the service** you signed up for: answer calls, run your workflows and show you call logs. Legal basis: our contract with you.
- **To bill you and keep records** that tax and company law require. Legal basis: legal obligation.
- **To support you, keep the service secure and prevent abuse,** including server and security logs. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure, reliable service.
- **To improve the product,** using aggregated or de-identified data where we can. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the service.

We need your account details to provide the service. Without them we cannot create an account for you. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

We do not sell personal data. We never use customer data, including call recordings and transcripts, to train AI models.

## 4. Google user data

You can connect a Google account so your agents can work with your calendar and spreadsheets. We only request access when you connect an integration, and only use it for the features you set up.

| Google service           | What Lightmove does with it                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google Calendar          | Check availability, and create or cancel events when a caller books.                  |
| Google Sheets            | Read and write rows in sheets you choose, for example to log calls or look up prices. |
| Email address            | Identify which Google account is connected.                                           |
| Google sign-in (if used) | Your name, email address and profile picture, to create your account and sign you in. |

Lightmove's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not let people read it unless you ask us to, it is needed for security, or the law requires it. You can disconnect at any time in the app or at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## 5. Demo calls on our website

If you press **Talk to Aoife** on our home page, you talk to an AI voice agent and we are the controller of that call. We record your voice and make a transcript to run the demo and to review how the agent performs, and we log technical data such as your IP address and browser. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by starting the call. You can withdraw it at any time: end the call, and email [privacy@lightmove.ai](mailto:privacy@lightmove.ai) with the date and time of your call to have the recording deleted. The audio is processed on Google Cloud in the EU. We keep demo recordings and transcripts for 30 days. Please do not share sensitive information on a demo call.

## 6. Who we share it with

We use these providers to run the service:

- **Google Cloud** for hosting in the EU, and for the platform's AI: Vertex AI for language models, and Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech for speech and voices. Under Google Cloud's terms, Google does not use this data to train its models.
- **Stripe** for payments.
- **Resend** for account emails such as sign-in links and invitations.
- **Telephony carriers** that connect phone calls to your agents.

Each of these providers is bound by a data processing agreement with us. Our [sub-processor list](/subprocessors) gives the details of each provider, where it processes data and how transfers are protected. If you connect your own account with another AI or observability provider, calls on your agents are processed by that provider under your agreement with it.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Call content:** recordings, transcripts and other call content are deleted automatically after 90 days. Each customer can set a shorter or longer period, from 1 to 365 days. To delete specific calls sooner, email us. We keep each call's date, duration, status and cost for billing.
- **Web widget and webhooks:** technical data from web widget sessions, such as IP addresses, and the content of completed webhook deliveries are deleted after 30 days.
- **Accounts:** account data is kept while your account is open. Email us to close your account, and we delete your account data within 30 days. Invoices and billing records are kept for six years, as Irish tax law requires.
- **Logs:** server and security logs are kept for [LOG RETENTION PERIOD].
- **Backups:** our database backups are deleted after 30 days, so deleted data leaves them within 30 days.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your personal data, and object to how we use it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email us to make a request. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the [Data Protection Commission](https://www.dataprotection.ie) in Ireland.

If you called a business that uses Lightmove, or were called by one, that business controls your call data and provided any contact details we hold for its campaigns. Contact them first. We will help them answer your request.

## 9. Security and transfers

Data is encrypted in transit between your browser or app and our servers, and encrypted at rest on Google Cloud. Calls on the public telephone network may not be encrypted by the carrier. Access is limited to staff who need it. The app and its data are hosted on Google Cloud in the EU (Belgium, europe-west1).

Some providers, such as Stripe and Resend, and our website's content delivery network may process data outside the EEA. Where they do, we rely on the safeguard each provider offers, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. Email us for a copy.
