This policy explains what personal data LightMove.ai collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers our website, the LightMove.ai app at app.lightmove.ai, and the voice agents our customers run on it.

LightMove.ai is operated by NextWebSpark [LEGAL ENTITY NAME, COMPANY NUMBER], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], Limerick, Ireland. For account and website data we are the data controller. For call data that our customers' agents handle, we act as a data processor on our customer's behalf.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, work email, company and login details.
- **Call data:** audio recordings, transcripts, caller phone numbers, call summaries and anything callers say to an agent.
- **Agent configuration:** the prompts, workflows and settings you create.
- **Billing details:** handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.
- **Usage data:** basic logs about how the website and app are used. [CONFIRM COOKIES AND ANALYTICS TOOLS]

## 3. How we use it

- To run the service: answer calls, run your workflows and show you call logs.
- To support you, bill you and keep the service secure.
- To improve the product, using aggregated or de-identified data where we can.

We do not sell personal data. [CONFIRM POLICY ON USING CUSTOMER DATA TO TRAIN MODELS]

## 4. Google user data

You can connect a Google account so your agents can work with your calendar, spreadsheets and email. We only request access when you connect an integration, and only use it for the features you set up.

| Google service           | What LightMove.ai does with it                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google Calendar          | Check availability and create, move or cancel events when a caller books.                     |
| Google Sheets            | Read and write rows in sheets you choose, for example to log calls or look up prices.          |
| Gmail (send only)        | Send emails you have set up in a workflow, such as call summaries. We cannot read your inbox. |
| Google Drive (app files) | Access only files LightMove.ai creates or you open with it.                                    |
| Email address            | Identify which Google account is connected.                                                   |

LightMove.ai's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not let people read it unless you ask us to, it is needed for security, or the law requires it. You can disconnect at any time in the app or at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
