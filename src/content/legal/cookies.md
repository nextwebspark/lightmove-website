## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Local storage and session storage work in a similar way. Under Irish law (the ePrivacy Regulations, S.I. No. 336 of 2011) a website must ask for your consent before it stores or reads anything on your device, unless it is strictly necessary for a service you asked for. The same rule covers tracking that works without cookies, such as tracking pixels or scripts that send details about your device, so we treat those the same way.

This policy is only about your browser. How we handle personal data more widely, including calls, call recordings and transcripts, and the AI that runs our voice agents, is in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 2. Our website

### What we use today

Today the website uses **no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies**, and no third-party tracking scripts. It only uses this storage, which is strictly necessary and needs no consent:

| Name            | Type          | Purpose                                                                                     | How long           |
| --------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| starlight-theme | Local storage | Remembers the light or dark theme you picked. It is only set when you use the theme switch. | Until you clear it |
| lm-consent      | Local storage | Remembers your cookie choices: which optional categories you allowed, and when.             | 180 days           |

### What we plan to add, only with your consent

We plan to add two kinds of optional tools. Neither runs yet. Our cookie banner asks for your choice now, and both stay off unless you allow them.

| Category         | What it would do                                                                                                           | Status         |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Analytics        | Count visits and see which pages are read, so we can improve the site.                                                     | Not in use yet |
| Error monitoring | Report errors in your browser, such as a page that fails to load, with technical details about the error and your browser. | Not in use yet |

Before we switch either on, we will update this page with the provider, what it collects, where the data is held and for how long, and **ask everyone again**, including anyone who has already chosen.

### Other things on the website

**Voice demo.** If you press **Talk to Aoife**, your browser loads our voice widget from app.lightmove.ai and connects your microphone to the call. The widget doesn't set cookies or browser storage. How we handle the call itself is in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy#5-demo-calls-on-our-website).

**Contact form.** Sending a message through our [contact form](/contact) sets no cookies.

**Server logs.** Like any website, our hosting provider logs requests, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the site. That isn't stored on your device; our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains it.

## 4. Changing your mind

On the website, use **Cookie settings** at the foot of the page (on the home page, the blog and these legal pages) to change your choices at any time. Rejecting is as easy as accepting. If you withdraw consent, we stop the tool from running from the next page you open.

## 5. Managing cookies in your browser

You can delete cookies and local storage, or block them, in your browser's settings. Blocking necessary storage may stop parts of the site, such as your theme choice or signing in to the app, from working.
