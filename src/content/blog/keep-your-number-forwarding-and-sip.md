Changing your phone number is a non-starter for most businesses. It's on your signage, your website and in your customers' contacts. The good news: you don't need to.

## Option 1: forward calls

The simplest setup. You get a number for your agent in LightMove.ai, then set your existing line to forward calls to it. Most providers let you choose when:

- **Always**, so the agent answers every call.
- **When busy or unanswered**, so the agent only picks up when your team can't.
- **Out of hours**, using your provider's time-based routing.

## Option 2: connect a SIP trunk

If you run a business phone system or work with a VoIP provider, you can point a SIP trunk at LightMove.ai. Calls arrive directly, without forwarding, and the agent can transfer back to extensions on your system.

## What to ask your provider

- Can I forward calls conditionally (busy, no answer, out of hours)?
- Do you support SIP trunks, and can I route a number to an external SIP address?
- Are there charges for forwarded minutes?
