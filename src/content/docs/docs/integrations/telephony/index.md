A telephony configuration connects your workspace to a phone provider. Add one under **Telephony** in the app: pick the provider, enter the account details it asks for, and save. Then add the phone numbers (or extensions) that should reach an agent, and choose an agent for each.

For forwarding an existing number or connecting a SIP trunk in general, see [Phone numbers and SIP](/docs/go-live/telephony).

## Providers

| Provider                                                  | Use it when                                                                |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Asterisk ARI](/docs/integrations/telephony/asterisk-ari) | You run your own Asterisk PBX and want calls handed to LightMove.ai agents |
| [Twilio](/docs/integrations/telephony/twilio)             | Your numbers are with Twilio                                               |
| [Telnyx](/docs/integrations/telephony/telnyx)             | Your numbers are with Telnyx                                               |
| [Vonage](/docs/integrations/telephony/vonage)             | Your numbers are with Vonage                                               |
| [Plivo](/docs/integrations/telephony/plivo)               | Your numbers are with Plivo                                                |
| [Exotel](/docs/integrations/telephony/exotel)             | Your numbers are with Exotel                                               |
| [Vobiz](/docs/integrations/telephony/vobiz)               | Your numbers are with Vobiz                                                |
| [Cloudonix](/docs/integrations/telephony/cloudonix)       | You use your own Cloudonix account and domain                              |
| [Managed SIP](/docs/integrations/telephony/dograh-sip)    | LightMove.ai provisions the SIP connection for you                         |

Each provider page lists exactly what the app asks for and where to find it in the provider's console.
