LightMove.ai lets you build voice agents that answer your phone and website calls, follow the steps you set, and hand over to your team when it matters.

## What an agent can do

- Answer calls on your existing number or on your website.
- Book, move and cancel appointments in your calendar.
- Answer questions about opening hours, prices and services.
- Take messages and book callbacks.
- Transfer the call to a person, with a summary.
- Log every call with a recording, transcript and summary.

## Three ways to start

1. **Try a template.** Pick a ready-made agent and talk to it in your browser. Free.
2. **Build your own.** Describe the agent in plain words and the [Agent Builder](/docs/build/agent-builder) drafts the workflow. Pay as you go.
3. **Have us build it.** Our team designs, connects and tests the agent for you. [Request a build](/#contact).
