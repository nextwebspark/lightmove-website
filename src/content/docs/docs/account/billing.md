LightMove.ai is pay as you go. There's no monthly commitment.

## What you pay for

- **Call minutes** — time your agents spend on calls, at €0.12 a minute, charged by the second.
- **Phone numbers and carrier charges** — where you use a number through LightMove.
- **Done-for-you builds** — a one-off quote, agreed before work starts.

New workspaces start with €5 of free credit, about 40 minutes of calls, so you can try templates and test calls in the browser before paying. Test calls use credit like any other call.

## Credit and top-ups

You pay in advance. Top up from €20 in **Settings → Billing**, and each call's cost comes off your credit when it ends. A call can only start with at least €0.50 of credit. Prices exclude VAT, which is added at checkout. [CONFIRM REFUND POLICY]
